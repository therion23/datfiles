## Aquaplus P/ECE
Origin: archive.org

Japanese RISC based handheld, originally meant as a PC games addon like the PocketStation and Dreamcast VMU. It shipped with a free SDK, so it became popular among hobbyists.

It suddenly became known in the rest of the world, when a couple of articles popped up on the net. Due to that, they are now extremely difficult to find on the second hand market.
