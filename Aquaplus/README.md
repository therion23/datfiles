## Aquaplus P/ECE
Origin: archive.org

Japanese RISC based handheld, originally meant as a PC games addon like the PocketStation and Dreamcast VMU. It shipped with a free SDK, so it became popular among hobbyists.

In the early 2020s, it suddenly became the talk of the rest of the world, when a couple of articles popped up on the net. Due to that, devices are now extremely difficult to find on the second hand market.

The Archive Sources dat is VERY much WIP - the rest should be okay as they are.
